Goa
Goa is a state in western India with coastlines stretching along the Arabian Sea.
Kerala
Kerala is a state on India's tropical Malabar Coast, known for its palm-lined beaches and backwaters.
Rajasthan
Rajasthan is a state in northern India known for its desert landscape, palaces, and forts.
Himachal Pradesh
Himachal Pradesh is a northern Indian state in the Himalayas known for its scenic mountain towns and resorts.
Uttarakhand
Uttarakhand is a state in northern India crossed by the Himalayas, known for its Hindu pilgrimage sites and natural beauty.
